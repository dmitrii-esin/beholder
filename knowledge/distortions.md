# Cognitive distortions (single source of truth)
Based on Beck and Burns. Name the distortion tentatively ("this might be…"), then ask for evidence.

| Distortion | Pattern | Example | Key question |
|---|---|---|---|
| All-or-nothing | Two categories only | "If it's not perfect, it's a failure." | "What's between 0 and 100?" |
| Overgeneralization | One event becomes a rule | "I always mess up." | "Always? What are the exceptions?" |
| Mental filter | Dwelling on one negative | One critical comment outweighs ten good ones | "What else happened?" |
| Disqualifying the positive | Positives "don't count" | "They were just being nice." | "What would count?" |
| Mind reading | Assuming what others think | "She thinks I'm boring." | "What's the evidence? Other explanations?" |
| Fortune telling | Predicting a bad outcome | "It'll go badly." | "What's most likely? Could you cope with the worst?" |
| Catastrophizing / magnification | Blowing up importance or consequences | "This ruins everything." | "How will it matter in a year?" |
| Minimization | Shrinking your own strengths or problems | "Anyone could do that." | "Would you say that about a friend?" |
| Emotional reasoning | Feeling = fact | "I feel stupid, so I am." | "What would you conclude without the feeling?" |
| Should statements | Rigid rules for self or others | "I should be over this." | "Says who? What's a preference version?" |
| Labeling | A global label from one act | "I'm a loser." | "Is that the whole person?" |
| Personalization / blame | Too much (or zero) responsibility | "It's my fault they're upset." | "What other factors contributed?" |

**Caution:** don't label accurate perceptions as distortions. When someone reports mistreatment, abuse or danger, validate it and problem-solve. Do not reframe it.
