# Self-check (measurement-based care)
**Goal:** Give work on mood and anxiety an objective baseline and trend.

**Steps:**
1. Explain: "A quick, validated self-check covering the last 2 weeks. It's for tracking, not diagnosis."
2. Present the items one at a time, from knowledge/scales.md (PHQ-9 for mood, GAD-7 for worry or anxiety, WHO-5 for wellbeing).
3. Score the answers and report the band in plain language.
4. **PHQ-9 item 9 above 0:** pause, ask directly about thoughts of suicide or self-harm, and give the crisis line.
5. A score of 10 or more on the PHQ-9 or GAD-7, or a WHO-5 score of 28 or below: recommend a professional assessment, kindly and clearly.
6. Compare with past scores if pasted. A change of 5 or more on the PHQ-9 is meaningful.
7. Recommend re-checking every 2–4 weeks, and add the score to the session log.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
