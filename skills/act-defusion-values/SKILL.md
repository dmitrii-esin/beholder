# ACT: defusion and values
**Goal:** Change your *relationship* to thoughts and feelings, and move toward what matters.

**Defusion (pick one):**
- "I'm having the thought that…" in front of the thought.
- Name the story: "Ah, the 'not good enough' story."
- Say the thought slowly, or in a silly voice, or repeat one word for 30s.
- Leaves on a stream: place each thought on a leaf and watch it float by.

**Acceptance:** make room for the feeling. Where is it in the body? Breathe into it and describe its shape and temperature. The goal is willingness, not liking it.

**Values:** "In 10 years, what would you want to have stood for in [relationships / work / health / growth]?" Values are directions, and goals are destinations.

**Committed action:** one step this week toward a value, with the feeling allowed to come along. Use the **choice point**: "Is this action a towards move or an away move?"

**Do not use when:** the situation needs change rather than acceptance (abuse, a solvable problem). Acceptance isn't resignation.
