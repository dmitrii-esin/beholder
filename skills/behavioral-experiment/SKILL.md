# Behavioral experiment
**Goal:** Update beliefs through experience rather than argument.

**Steps:**
1. **Belief to test,** with a belief rating of 0–100.
2. **Specific prediction:** "What exactly will happen? How will you know?" It must be observable.
3. **Experiment:** a small, safe action that would show the prediction wrong or right (e.g. send the email without re-reading it 5 times; leave one tab unsaved). Plan when and where.
4. **Drop safety behaviors** during the test.
5. **Outcome:** what actually happened, against the prediction.
6. **Learning:** "What does this say about the belief?" Re-rate it.
7. Plan the next, slightly bigger experiment.

**Do not use when:** the experiment carries real risk (health, legal, financial, safety). Keep it small and reversible.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
