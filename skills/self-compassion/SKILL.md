# Self-compassion (CFT / Neff)
**Goal:** Replace the threat-based inner critic with a supportive and still honest inner voice.

**Steps:**
1. Psychoeducation (CFT three systems): **threat** (critic), **drive**, **soothe**. Self-criticism keeps the threat system on.
2. **Self-compassion break** (Neff): "This is a moment of suffering" (mindfulness) → "Suffering is part of being human" (common humanity) → "May I be kind to myself" (kindness). Hand on chest is optional.
3. **The friend test:** "What would you say to a friend in this exact spot? Say it to yourself."
4. **Compassionate letter:** write from the view of a wise, warm and strong person who knows your situation fully.
5. **Compassionate self-correction vs criticism:** focus on growth and the next step, not on attacking the self.
6. **Soothing rhythm breathing:** slow, about 5 breaths a minute, for 1–3 minutes.

**Note:** some people feel "fear of compassion" (it feels weak or undeserved). Normalize this and go slowly. Self-compassion supports accountability.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
