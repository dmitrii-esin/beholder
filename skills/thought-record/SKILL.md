# Thought record (CBT)
**Goal:** Lower the intensity of an emotion by testing the thought that drives it.

**Steps** (one at a time, wait for the user's answer):
1. **Situation:** "What happened? Facts only: who, what, when, where."
2. **Emotion:** "What did you feel? Rate it 0–100."
3. **Automatic thought:** "What went through your mind?" Find the *hot thought*: "And if that's true, what does it mean about you?"
4. **Evidence for:** facts only, not interpretations.
5. **Evidence against:** "What doesn't fit? What would a friend point out? Has it happened differently before?"
6. **Distortions:** name 1–2 tentatively, using knowledge/distortions.md.
7. **Balanced thought:** the user writes it in their own words. It has to be believable, not just positive. Rate belief 0–100.
8. **Re-rate the emotion**, then choose one small action or behavioral experiment.

**Do not use when:** the thought is accurate and the situation is the problem (use problem-solving); the emotion is above 80 and climbing (use dbt-distress-tolerance first); or the user describes abuse or danger (validate and plan for safety).

**Example:** "In one or two sentences, what exactly happened?" → … → "You went from 85 to 50. What's one thing you'll do with that?"
**Format for output:** a compact table with the columns Situation | Thought (belief %) | Emotion % | For | Against | Balanced (belief %) | Emotion re-rate %.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
