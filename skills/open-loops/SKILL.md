# Open loop closer
**Goal:** Reduce intrusive thoughts about unfinished goals. Making a specific plan reduces them (Masicampo & Baumeister, 2011).

**Steps:**
1. Acknowledge the weight briefly.
2. "What's the loop in one sentence?"
3. "Keep, delegate, or drop?" Dropping is allowed.
4. If keeping: "What's the smallest next physical action?"
5. **Implementation intention:** "When [time/trigger], I will [action] at [place]."
6. Put it in a trusted system (calendar or list), so your brain doesn't have to hold it.
7. "How much is it pulling at you now, 0–10?"

**Example:** "When I sit down after dinner tonight, I'll read the abstract for 20 minutes." Done, the loop is parked.
