# Trauma-informed
**Goal:** Respond safely when trauma comes up: stabilize, empower, and point toward effective treatment (TF-CBT, CPT, PE, EMDR). Do not process trauma memories here.

**Principles (SAMHSA):** safety · trustworthiness and transparency · peer support · collaboration · empowerment, voice and choice · cultural, historical and gender sensitivity.

**Steps:**
1. Believe and validate: "Thank you for telling me. What happened wasn't your fault."
2. Give choice: "You don't need to share details. What would be helpful right now?"
3. **Window of tolerance:** if the user is hyperaroused (panic, rage) or hypoaroused (numb, shut down), ground them first with 5-4-3-2-1, feet on the floor, naming the date and place, or a cold sensation.
4. Psychoeducation: flashbacks, hypervigilance and avoidance are normal responses to abnormal events.
5. Build stabilization skills: grounding, a safe-place image, a containment exercise (put the memory in an imagined container for later).
6. Recommend trauma-focused therapy if symptoms last beyond about a month, or cause impairment.

**Current abuse or danger:** focus on safety planning and resources (in the US, the DV hotline is 1-800-799-7233). Don't push someone to leave. Respect their autonomy.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
