# Sleep (CBT-I basics)
**Goal:** Rebuild the bed–sleep association and sleep drive. CBT-I is the first-line treatment for chronic insomnia. Sleep hygiene alone isn't enough.

**Steps:**
1. **Sleep diary for 1–2 weeks:** bedtime, sleep-onset time, awakenings, wake time, out-of-bed time, sleep quality. Calculate sleep efficiency (SE) = time asleep ÷ time in bed.
2. **Stimulus control:** go to bed only when sleepy. If awake for about 20 minutes (estimate, don't watch the clock), get up and do something dull in dim light, then return. The bed is for sleep and sex only. Keep a fixed wake time every day, weekends included. No naps, or one under 20 minutes before 3pm.
3. **Sleep restriction / compression:** set time in bed to the average actual sleep, never under 5.5 hours, with the wake time fixed. Each week, if SE ≥ 85–90%, add 15 minutes; if SE < 80–85%, reduce by 15. Expect daytime sleepiness early on, so don't drive when drowsy.
4. **Cognitive:** challenge catastrophic sleep thoughts ("If I don't sleep 8h I'll fail tomorrow"). Note that you've coped before. Paradoxical intention: try to stay awake, gently.
5. **Wind-down:** 30–60 minutes away from work and screens. Use a worry slot earlier in the evening (worry-postponement).
6. **Basics:** caffeine none after about 2pm, limit alcohol, get morning light.

**Do not use sleep restriction with** bipolar disorder (risk of mania), epilepsy, sleep apnea symptoms (loud snoring, gasping, daytime sleepiness: see a doctor), pregnancy, or jobs where drowsiness is dangerous. Insomnia lasting over 3 months: suggest a CBT-I provider.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
