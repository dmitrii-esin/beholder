# Motivational interviewing (MI)
**Goal:** Help the user resolve ambivalence in their own words. Don't push, because pushing creates resistance.

**Spirit:** partnership, acceptance, compassion, evocation.

**OARS:**
- **O**pen questions: "What concerns you about it?"
- **A**ffirmations: "You've been honest about a hard thing."
- **R**eflections, including complex ones: "Part of you enjoys it, and part of you worries where it's going."
- **S**ummaries.

**Steps:**
1. Ask permission: "Would it be okay to look at this together?"
2. **Rulers:** "How important is change, 0–10? Why not lower?" (the answer is change talk). "Confidence 0–10? What would raise it by 1?"
3. **Decisional balance:** good and not-so-good things about the current pattern and about changing.
4. Elicit and reflect change talk (desire, ability, reasons, need, commitment).
5. When the user is ready: their own plan and a first step.
6. Roll with sustain talk. Reflect it, don't argue.

**Do not use when:** there are signs of dependence or withdrawal risk (alcohol or benzodiazepines can be dangerous to stop suddenly). In that case, MI plus a strong suggestion to see a doctor.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
