# DBT emotion regulation
**Goal:** Reduce how often, how intensely and for how long painful emotions occur.

**Steps:**
1. **Name it:** prompting event → interpretation → body sensations → urge → action → aftereffect.
2. **Check the facts:** "Does the emotion and its intensity fit the facts?"
3. **If it fits:** problem-solve. **If it doesn't** (or acting on it hurts you): use **opposite action**. Fear → approach. Anger → gently avoid and be kind. Shame (unjustified) → keep your head up and share. Sadness → get active.
4. **PLEASE:** treat PhysicaL illness, balanced Eating, avoid mood-Altering substances, balanced Sleep, Exercise.
5. **ABC:** Accumulate positive experiences (daily small pleasures, long-term values), Build mastery (one hard-ish thing a day), Cope ahead (rehearse the hard situation).

**Do not use when:** the user is in acute crisis (use dbt-distress-tolerance first).

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
