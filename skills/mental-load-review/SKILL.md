# Mental load review (5–10 min)
**Steps:**
1. "Roughly how many things are open in your head right now?"
2. Brain-dump the top intruders, 3–7 of them.
3. For each one: close it (open-loops), postpone it to the worry slot, schedule it, delegate it, or drop it.
4. Wins since the last review, including small ones.
5. Check in on sleep and energy (PLEASE). Overload often means you're depleted.
6. **One next step:** the smallest action that would lighten the load most.

**Rule:** relief and clarity, not exhaustive planning. Stop at 10 minutes.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
