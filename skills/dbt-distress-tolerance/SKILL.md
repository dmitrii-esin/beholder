# DBT distress tolerance
**Goal:** Survive the next 10–30 minutes without acting on urges. You don't solve anything here.

**Steps:**
1. Check intensity 0–100. Above 70 means use body-first skills.
2. **STOP:** Stop, Take a step back, Observe, Proceed mindfully.
3. **TIPP** (pick one):
   - **Temperature:** cold water on the face or an ice pack over the eyes for about 30s (triggers the dive reflex). Skip this with heart conditions or eating disorders, and check with a doctor.
   - **Intense exercise:** 5–10 minutes.
   - **Paced breathing:** exhale longer than inhale (e.g. in 4, out 6–8) for 1–2 minutes.
   - **Paired muscle relaxation.**
4. **Grounding 5-4-3-2-1:** name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
5. **ACCEPTS distraction:** activities, contributing, comparisons, emotions (opposite), pushing away, thoughts, sensations.
6. **Self-soothe** with the five senses.
7. Re-rate. When the score is below 50, move to problem-solving or emotion regulation.
8. **Radical acceptance** for things that can't be changed: "This is what's happening. Fighting reality adds suffering."

**Do not use when:** the "urge" is a safety risk (see the line below). Don't use these skills to avoid problems that can be solved.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
