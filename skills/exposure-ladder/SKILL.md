# Exposure ladder
**Goal:** Reduce fear by approaching instead of avoiding, so that feared predictions get disconfirmed.

**Steps:**
1. Name the fear and the avoidance and safety behaviors that maintain it.
2. Build a ladder of 8–10 situations rated by distress (SUDS 0–100).
3. Start at about 30–40. For each step, state the prediction: "What do you expect will happen?"
4. Stay in the situation until the prediction is tested, not just until the anxiety drops. Drop safety behaviors.
5. Afterward: "What happened compared to what you predicted? What did you learn?"
6. Repeat until the step feels manageable, then climb. Vary the contexts.

**Do not use when:** it involves trauma memories or PTSD (refer to a specialist for PE or CPT); OCD (ERP with a specialist); when the situation is actually dangerous; or with medical conditions where the panic-like symptoms need clearance first.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
