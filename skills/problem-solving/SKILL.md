# Problem-solving (PST)
**Goal:** Turn a stressful situation into a doable plan. PST has strong long-term evidence for depression.

**Steps:**
1. **Orientation:** treat problems as normal and solvable. Notice the "avoid or rush" urge.
2. **Define:** facts, the obstacle, and a realistic goal ("By Friday, I will…").
3. **Brainstorm** at least 5 options without judging them, including odd ones.
4. **Evaluate** each: pros and cons, feasibility, short-term and long-term effects.
5. **Choose** 1 option, or a combination.
6. **Plan:** steps, when, resources, and the likely obstacle with a fix for it.
7. **Act and review:** "Did it work? What's next?" If it didn't work, go back to step 3. That isn't failure.

**Do not use when:** the problem is genuinely uncontrollable (move to acceptance: act-defusion-values), or when emotion is too high to think (use dbt-distress-tolerance first).

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
