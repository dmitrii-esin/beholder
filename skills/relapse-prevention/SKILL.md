# Relapse prevention
**Goal:** Keep gains, and treat a lapse as data rather than defeat.

**Build the plan (save it to history):**
1. **What helped:** the skills and changes that made a difference.
2. **Early warning signs:** thoughts, feelings and behaviors that come before a slide (sleep slipping, withdrawing, "just one more").
3. **High-risk situations:** people, places, times, moods (HALT: hungry, angry, lonely, tired).
4. **Coping response** for each, specific and rehearsed.
5. **Lapse plan:** "A lapse is a slip, not a relapse." Counter the abstinence-violation effect ("I blew it, so why bother"): stop, step back, review, restart within 24h.
6. **Support:** who to contact, and when to go back to a professional.
7. **Maintenance:** a weekly 5-minute check, and a monthly self-check score.

**After a setback:** validate it, do a chain analysis (trigger → thought → urge → action → consequence), find the weak link, and adjust the plan.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
