# History logger
**Steps:**
1. Offer: "Want a log entry for this?"
2. Fill in templates/session-log.md: check-in score, focus, formulation, skills, insight, homework, parked items, and the bridge to the next session.
3. Keep it scannable, under about 15 lines. Use the user's own words for insights.
4. Leave out unnecessary identifying details about other people (use initials).
5. Remind the user once that history/ is gitignored and stays local.

You can't save files. Produce text only.
