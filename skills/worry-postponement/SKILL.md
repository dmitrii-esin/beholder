# Worry postponement
**Goal:** Learn by experience that worry can be postponed and that it often fades.

**Setup (once):** choose a daily 15-minute slot, at the same time and in the same place, not within 2 hours of bedtime.

**In the moment:**
1. Validate in one line: "Worry showed up."
2. Practical check: "Is there one action you can take today?" If yes, do it or schedule it, and postpone the rest.
3. Write the worry in a single line, then return attention to the present task. Detached mindfulness (MCT): notice the thought like a passing car, without engaging with it or pushing it away.

**In the worry slot:**
4. Review each item and sort it: solvable → one next step (problem-solving); unsolvable or uncertain → acknowledge and let go; faded → cross off.
5. Stop on time.

**Review weekly:** "What percentage of worries had faded by the slot? What does that say about your belief that worry is uncontrollable?"

**Do not use when:** the thought is a real problem due today; it is trauma intrusion (trauma-informed); or it is an OCD-style doubt that needs checking or reassurance, because postponement can become a ritual and needs ERP with a specialist.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
