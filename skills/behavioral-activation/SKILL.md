# Behavioral activation (BA)
**Goal:** Break the cycle of low mood → withdrawal → lower mood. Action comes before motivation.

**Steps:**
1. Psychoeducation in 2 lines: "When we're low we do less, then feel worse. We'll do things *before* we feel like it."
2. **Monitor** for 2–3 days: hourly or block-by-block activity, with mood 0–10, a sense of achievement (A 0–10) and pleasure (P 0–10).
3. **Values:** pick 1–2 life areas (relationships, health, work, play) and name small activities in each.
4. **Schedule:** 1–3 activities per day, with a day, time and place. Grade them: "Walk 10 minutes" before "go to the gym". Mix routine (shower, meals), pleasant and necessary activities.
5. **Troubleshoot avoidance:** map trigger → avoidance → short-term relief → long-term cost (the TRAP to TRAC pattern: trigger, response, alternative coping).
6. **Review:** compare predicted vs actual mood, then adjust difficulty.

**Do not use when:** physical illness explains the fatigue (suggest a medical check). If mood is severe (PHQ-9 ≥ 20) or functioning is collapsing, use BA *and* recommend a professional.

---
If suicide or self-harm comes up: pause the technique, ask directly about safety, and give 988 (US call/text), 112 / local emergency, or findahelpline.com.
